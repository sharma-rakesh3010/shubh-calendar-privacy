Shubh Calendar Privacy Policy

Developed by Workbox Pro Apps

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Shubh Calendar, developed by Workbox Pro Apps. Shubh Calendar is a Hindu calendar application designed to provide daily Panchang details, Hindu festivals, reminders, important dates, and devotional content.

Shubh Calendar is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in, does not ask for your name, phone number, or email address, and does not operate an app-specific backend server to collect or store your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally on your device and how integrated third-party services process technical, diagnostic, advertising, analytics, notification, and purchase-related information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Shubh Calendar allows you to create and manage personal content directly on your device.

  • Reminders and Special Days: Titles, dates, times, categories, repetition settings, notification and alarm preferences, optional notes, and Hindu calendar criteria such as tithi, paksha, or maas are stored locally.
  • Preferences: Your selected language, theme mode, display options, notification schedules, and manually selected Panchang city are stored locally.

Personal content and settings are stored in the device's private application storage using SQLite databases through Android Room and SharedPreferences.

Android backup rules are configured to exclude these local databases and SharedPreferences from automatic backup. Your locally stored app data is therefore not automatically uploaded by Shubh Calendar to Google Drive or an app-specific cloud server.

Clearing app data or uninstalling Shubh Calendar removes the locally stored app data. Any backup file that you manually exported remains wherever you chose to save it and must be deleted separately if you no longer want to keep it.

3. Notifications and Alarms

Shubh Calendar provides reminders, alarms, and daily Panchang notifications using Android notification and alarm capabilities.

  • Notifications: On supported Android versions, notification permission is used to display reminders, calendar notifications, and daily Panchang alerts.
  • Exact Alarms: On supported Android versions, exact alarm capability may be used to trigger scheduled reminders at the requested time.
  • Boot Completed: Used to restore or reschedule appropriate reminder and alarm schedules after the device restarts.
  • Vibration: Used for notification and alarm haptic feedback.
  • Full-Screen Intent: Where permitted by Android, this may be used to display an alarm interface when an alarm fires.
  • Battery Optimization: The app may request appropriate battery settings so that time-sensitive reminders are less likely to be delayed by the operating system.

Shubh Calendar does not access your personal device calendar provider and does not transmit your reminder titles, notes, or alarm details to an app-specific server.

4. Analytics

Shubh Calendar uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand application usage, improve features, and maintain the user experience.

Information processed may include:

  • Feature and screen interaction events
  • Session duration and application usage information
  • App version
  • Selected language
  • Theme selection
  • Pro entitlement status
  • Notification-related settings
  • Pseudonymous application identifiers and technical information

Shubh Calendar does not intentionally send reminder titles, descriptions, personal notes, email addresses, phone numbers, or user-entered search text to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics processes information according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

5. Crash Reporting

Shubh Calendar uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose software errors, monitor stability, and improve application reliability.

When crashes or handled application errors occur, technical diagnostic information may be processed, including stack traces, device manufacturer and model, Android version, SDK level, app version, screen information, language, theme, and error categories.

Crash reports are not intended to contain your personal identity, reminder contents, personal notes, or contact information.

6. Performance Monitoring

Shubh Calendar uses Google Firebase Performance Monitoring to understand application responsiveness, startup performance, resource loading, and other technical performance characteristics.

Performance information may include operation duration, technical metrics, item counts, cache information, screen names, repository information, language, theme, and related technical attributes.

Shubh Calendar does not intentionally attach personal information, user-entered text, or reminder contents to performance traces.

7. Advertising

The free version of Shubh Calendar displays banner advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ads are displayed on selected screens of the application.

  • How ads are served: Shubh Calendar initiates banner ad requests through the Google Mobile Ads SDK using standard ad requests. The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit your Advertising ID, GPS location, or personal user profile.
  • Third-party processing: Google AdMob may process technical device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and diagnostic or technical signals as necessary for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
  • Ad preferences: You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID and advertising preferences using the relevant Google/Android device settings.
  • Ad-free experience: You can permanently remove advertisements by purchasing Shubh Calendar Pro. When Pro is active, the app suppresses its advertisements and does not initiate banner ad requests.

Where applicable, Google consent-management mechanisms may be used to manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

8. Google Play Billing

Shubh Calendar offers an optional one-time in-app purchase called Shubh Calendar Pro, product ID shubh_calendar_pro, which permanently removes advertisements.

Payment processing and payment method information are handled by Google Play. Shubh Calendar does not receive or store your credit card number, debit card number, bank account information, or billing address.

The app verifies the purchase entitlement through Google Play Billing on the device. Shubh Calendar does not operate an external server for storing purchase tokens.

9. YouTube Content

The Devotional section may include embedded YouTube playback for devotional content such as Aartis, Chalisas, and Stotras.

The app passes the public YouTube video identifier associated with the selected content to the YouTube player integration.

Shubh Calendar does not require you to sign in to YouTube, does not access your YouTube account or credentials, and does not store YouTube login information.

YouTube playback is subject to YouTube Terms of Service and Google's privacy practices.

10. Backup and Restore

Shubh Calendar includes a manual Backup and Restore feature that allows you to safeguard and transfer reminders and special days.

  • User initiated: Backup export and restore occur only when you explicitly choose to use the feature.
  • Storage: Android's Storage Access Framework allows you to choose where a backup file is saved or from where it is opened.
  • Content: The exported JSON backup contains saved reminder and special-day information supported by the backup feature.
  • No automatic cloud synchronization: Shubh Calendar does not automatically upload or synchronize your backup file to an app-specific server.

Once you export a backup, the security and deletion of that exported file are under your control and depend on the storage location you selected.

11. Firebase Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Shubh Calendar uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver application notifications.

The app may use Firebase-managed installation and messaging identifiers required for notification delivery. Shubh Calendar does not upload your name, email address, phone number, reminder contents, or personal notes to an app-specific server for FCM.

The application may subscribe users to general notification topics used for Shubh Calendar notifications. Notification delivery is handled by Firebase/Google infrastructure.

Firebase In-App Messaging

Shubh Calendar may use Firebase In-App Messaging to display contextual messages while the app is being used.

Messaging may use Firebase Analytics information and relevant application properties, such as language, app engagement, or Pro entitlement status, to determine which in-app messages should be displayed.

14. Security, Retention and Deletion

Security

Locally stored reminders, special days, and preferences are protected by Android's private application storage and sandbox.

Network communications initiated by integrated third-party services use secure transport mechanisms such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by those services.

Local Data Retention

Locally stored reminders, special days, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove them using the app's available controls.

Externally Processed Data

Analytics, crash reporting, performance, advertising, notification, and other technical data processed by third-party services is retained and handled by those services according to their respective policies and retention practices.

Data Deletion Requests

Because Shubh Calendar does not have an app-specific user account system or backend user database, there is no Shubh Calendar account to delete.

You can delete locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application. Manually exported backup files must be deleted separately from the storage location where you saved them.
